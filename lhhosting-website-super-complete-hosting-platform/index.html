FAQ

Questions before choosing a plan.

Which hosting type should I choose?+

Shared hosting is simplest for small sites; WordPress plans optimize that CMS workflow; VPS and cloud give more control; dedicated servers are for workloads that need reserved hardware.

Are the prices final?+

No. This rebuilt site uses demo pricing until real commercial plans are verified.

Can LHHosting migrate an existing site?+

The prototype includes assisted migration workflows and eligibility language. Final scope should be confirmed operationally.

Do you include SSL and backups?+

Some sample plans show bundled capabilities while SSL and backup products are also available separately in the catalog.
