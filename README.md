## About Lhhosting

**Lhhosting** is a **Nonprofit** in **Portland** — Community work funded transparently—donors see where money goes.

We publish hours, offerings and contact paths clearly so customers know what to expect before they visit or book.

---

## Why we exist

Lhhosting exists to make nonprofit feel personal in Portland — clear pricing, honest recommendations, and a team that remembers why you came back.

- Rooted in Portland with regulars who know our name
- Offerings that match what we publish online
- Support you can reach during real hours
- A space built for repeat visits, not one-offs

---

## Where we're headed

We're growing Lhhosting into a community people recommend without hesitation — more depth in our craft, same straightforward experience at the door.
